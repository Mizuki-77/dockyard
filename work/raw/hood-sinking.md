## 2. 争议逐条展开

### 争议一：弹药库爆炸的直接原因——那一发 38 cm 弹走了什么路径

这是整个争议的核心。学界对「俾斯麦号一发 38 cm 弹导致后部弹药库爆炸」没有分歧；分歧全在**弹道**上。

#### 1.1 命中水线位置（相对哪个肋骨／段）

| 主张者 | 位置 | 依据／原话 |
|---|---|---|
| **D.E.J. Offord**（R.C.N.C.，海军建造副总监，1941-09-04 在 Walker 委员会作证） | 一条约 **18 英寸高**的窄带，「**extending from bulkhead 259 to bulkhead 280**」 | 「By searching with the pointer on the model one can find a small area in which a shell can enter and pass over the top of the 12" belt and yet get in under the flat portion of the protective main deck.」→「the zone is about 18" deep extending from bulkhead 259 to bulkhead 280」 |
| **William J. Jurens**（1987/1999，INRO） | 同一条带，但给出宽度：「**about 450mm wide located slightly above the probable waterline, and stretching from frame 259 to frame 280, i.e., abreast the after engine room**」 | 肋骨间距约 640 mm |

两说在**位置**上一致（259–280 站，后机舱侧方、主装带上缘之上），差别只在带宽（18 in ≈ 457 mm vs 450 mm）——几乎是同一个数。这是本争议里最难得的一致点。

#### 1.2 是否穿透主装甲带

**没有任何一个严肃主张认为那一发「干净地穿透了 305 mm 主装带」。** 争论的是「从主装带上缘与装甲甲板之间的接缝钻进去」还是「从主装带下缘入水、走水下弹道」。

- **Offord（1941）** 给出两组互相矛盾的德弹初速数据，直接决定结论：
  - Case A（2721 f.s.，俄方情报来源）：**打不穿**主装带。「her shell would defeat only 10½ [inch] Cemented armour. Thus no such shell could defeat HOOD'S main belt.」结论「a very lucky shell … I would, in fact say, that it is not impossible, but highly improbable.」
  - Case B（3150 f.s.，D.N.O. 弹道处的「外推上限」）：**能打穿**。「BISMARCK's shell would defeat 14" of cemented armour at 16,500 yards and 50° inclination. Thus HOOD's main belt and the plating behind could be defeated and with a very considerable delay a shell could enter and burst in, or alternatively burst close to, any of the main or 4" magazines.」
  - 临界初速：「**The muzzle velocity would be about 3050 f.s.**」
- **Woollerton**（D.N.O. 部引信专家，1941-09-05）承认 2721 这个数是俄方情报：「The figure 2721 was taken from a secret report sent to the Admiralty by the Russian Intelligence. This figure is not considered to be entirely reliable as other figures given in the same message have been shown to be incorrect. The higher figure was an outside estimation by D.N.O.'S Ballistic Department of what was considered possible.」——**也就是说 1941 年英国人手头根本没有可靠的德弹初速**，这是整场争论的技术根源。
- **Jurens（1987）** 用自己的弹道重建算出：命中时距离约 18,100 m，目标角 53°（转身前）／约 73°（完成转身），**着角 43.85°（≈44°）／28–29°**，着速约 530 mps；对 face-hardened 装甲的穿透力约 **240 mm**，因此 **305 mm 主装带的完整穿透「improbable」**；但 127 mm 上带、178 mm 中带容易被击穿（出速 450 / 365 mps）。
- **Walker 委员会结论段**：「Expert evidence shows that this is quite possible if the muzzle velocity of Bismarck's shell was between 2721 and 3050 fps. If the muzzle velocity was over 3050 fps, the probability, bearing in mind the evidence as to the fall of shot, is considerable.」

**学界现状**：现有主流表述既不说「穿透主装带」，也不说「穿甲板」，而是「从主装带上缘的窄缝钻入」或「从主装带下缘入水后走水下弹道」——两条通道都需要「a very lucky shell」。

一个必须注意的**内部矛盾**：Jurens 1987 年明确说甲板落角约 10.6°–13.9°，对甲板的穿深只有约 65 mm 而需要 120 mm，**否定穿甲板**；但 2019 年 Jurens / Garzke / Dulin / Roberts / Fiske 的 SNAME 论文（经 Wikipedia 转述）却改口为「penetrated the deck armour and exploded in the aft 4-inch magazine」。这两说不能同时成立，属于作者本人的后期修正，报告里应并列呈现。

#### 1.3 引爆的是发射药还是弹头

**发射药，不是弹头装药。** 这是全场共识最强的一条，依据是目击者「没听见响声」。

- **Jurens 的原话**（这是他全文最关键的推理）：「The 'bang' of an explosion is caused by the creation of a supersonic shockwave, and is necessarily absent when burning occurs, even very rapid burning indeed. The noiselessness of the explosion constitutes strong circumstantial evidence that the fatal blast was caused not by the detonation of Hood's shells or torpedoes but by the more or less rapid combustion of her propellants.」
- **Walker 委员会**：「**The large majority of witnesses heard no noise of the explosion.**」
- **Kennedy 中尉（Norfolk）**：「Did you see any flash of the explosion? No, that was one thing I could not understand at the time.」
- **Tilburn**：「No, Sir, there was dead silence after the explosion.」
- **Briggs**：「it was not a terrific explosion at all as regards noise」
- **Dundas**：「There was no blast bigger than that of the ship's own guns firing.」
- 爆炸 → 是**发射药燃烧（deflagration）而非爆轰（detonation）**。美舰亚利桑那号弹药库爆炸有同样的「无声」特征，Jurens 特意点出这个类比。

#### 1.4 Jurens 的流体力学／弹道重建

- **水下弹道**：38 cm 弹正常入水后大约穿 **6 倍口径（约 2.28 m）**深。对照组是 PoW 挨的那发 dud——「One of BISMARCK'S 15" shell entered the outer bottom of the PRINCE OF WALES just at the upper edge of her bilge keel… The total travel from the time when this shell struck the water until it was brought to rest within the ship was about 90 ft. The shell was 'blind.'」Jurens 说这一发深入水线下 8.5 m，属「**remarkable**」，推测是 windscreen 脱落后 knob-and-ring 被帽形成 **stagnation cup**（滞止杯）改变了入水姿态。
- **引信延迟**：0.035 s 约等于空中 19 m／水下 13–14 m 的行程。
- **排水／排气路径**（他解释「为什么火柱出现在主桅」的关键）：燃气经主桅两侧的机舱排气道上升，在 boat deck 上开口「roughly square vents **1.8 meters on a side**」。**目击者看到的「主桅处火柱」是燃气从排气管喷出，不是爆炸中心在主桅**——这正好化解了 DNC 当年那个「主桅下方没有弹药库」的反对。
- **弹药量**：「'X' 15-in magazine contained about 49 tons of cordite, 'Y' magazine contained 45 tons, and the 4-in magazines contained about 18.5 tons」（合计约 112.5 吨）。Walker 委员会的对比句：「our peace time knowledge and practical experience of the results of cordite explosion has been based on experiments with a maximum of about half a ton of cordite. **Hood's after magazine contained about 112 tons, over two hundred times as much.**」
- **结论**：「I have concluded that it is most probable that Hood was relatively slowly rent apart over a period of perhaps a second by the uncontrolled burning of the propellants in her after magazines… the most probable cause of the final blast was **a hit from a single 380mm projectile from Bismarck**.」
- **发射药敏感性**：英制 cordite 在距火焰 530 mm 即点燃，美制单基药需 120 mm。他的原话：「Had Hood carried single base propellant instead of cordite, there is in fact a good possibility that the fatal explosion might never have occurred.」

#### 1.5 2001 年 Mearns / Blue Water Recoveries 远征的发现

（**不是 1999 年 Ballard**，见 §0。）

- 2001-07-19 由 Ocean Explorer 6000 侧扫声呐在第 39 小时找到，水深约 2,845 m（9,334 ft）。
- 位置「vicinity of 63°22'N 032°17'W」，精确坐标未公布。
- 残骸分三大部分 + 两个大碎片区：舰首（约 30.5 m，左倾卧）、中段（≥100 m，倒扣在撞击坑里）、舰尾（约 38 m 分两段）。
- **最硬的证据：舵被蜗杆锁在约 20° 左转位置**。官方 Findings 原话：「Confirmation that Hood had definitely been in the midst of her final 20° turn to port. Prior to this, many people, to include the members of the second Board of Inquiry into the loss of Hood, doubted that this final turn to port was ever executed.」——这推翻了 1941 年 Walker 委员会的一个怀疑。
- **确认后部弹药库爆炸**，但**没找到「冒烟的枪」**：「Confirmation that Hood was destroyed by a large conflagration/explosion in her aft magazines. Though it is true that no 'smoking gun' (such as a telltale 15"/38cm shell hole in some external plating) was found, the damage fits what one would expect from a conflagration/explosion of 112 or more tons of cordite. **There are alternative possibilities, but a shell from Bismarck still appears to be the most likely culprit.**」
- 碎片清单里有大信息量：「crockery, boots, a HACS Mk III* director, engine/propulsion parts, both forward/outboard screws, propeller shafting, both rear main gun barbettes, a 4" gun mount, a 0.5" machine gun pedestal, half of a torpedo, electrical gear/wiring, the intact foremast and starfish platform, much of the after superstructure, a bell, pieces of the forward breakwater, **large quantities of 4" brass cartridges (minus their warheads)**」。
- 中段右舷「missing down to the inner wall of the fuel tanks and the plates of the hull are curling outward」→ 被判读为爆炸泄压路径。
- **舰首的灾难性损伤是最大意外**，两派解释：
  - Mearns / Eric Grove：前部弹药库也发生了（部分）爆炸。
  - Jurens 反对：「there was no magazine of any kind at the location of the break and that the location of the break just forward of the forward transverse armoured bulkhead suggests that the ship's structure failed there as a result of stresses inflicted when the bow was lifted into the vertical position by the sinking stern section. Furthermore, the current position of the plates at the edge of the break reflects only their last position, not the direction they had first moved.」
  - 第三方（维基记）：「a team of marine forensic scientists has found that implosion damage to the forward hull due to the rapid sinking of the Hood, is the most likely cause of the state of the forward hull, and they do not support any theory that the forward magazines exploded.」

**学界现状**：2001 年考察**没能裁决弹道问题**。它确认了三件事——后部弹药库炸了、舵锁在左转位、舰体断成三段——但对「那一发弹从哪儿进去的」明确说没有 smoking gun。

#### 1.6 1941 年 DNC 的反驳：一个至今没被彻底驳倒的技术异议

这点任务书没要求，但它是理解争议结构的关键，必须写进来。**Director of Naval Construction, S.V. Goodall 1941-07-02 备忘录**（ADM 116/4351）直接反对弹药库说，理由极其锋利：

- 「The result of the fifth salvo was a large explosion, the centre of which appeared to be at the base of the mainmast.」→「It is extremely difficult to associate this observed fact with the explosion of the 4" magazines, the forward bulkhead of which is 64ft. abaft the centre of the mainmast and the after bulkhead about 115ft. If the 4" magazines caused 'X' magazine to blow up, a fortiori, 'X' magazine would have caused 'Y' magazine to blow up. The after bulkhead of 'Y' magazine is about 180 ft. abaft the centre of the mainmast.」
- 4 英寸弹药库群是 6 个独立水密舱，上方有 7 个舱口／围井泄压，若它们炸，火应该出现在上层建筑后端、X 炮塔之前，**不可能绕过艉机舱跑到主桅**：「Is there any possible reason why the products of combustion from these magazines should have passed through the after engine-room and up round the mainmast over the centre engine room? No reason is indicated in the report, neither can one be imagined.」
- 他的替代假说：上甲板主桅侧方约 4,000 lbs TNT（8 个鱼雷战斗部）被第 5 次齐射击中引爆 →「such a detonation in the position of these tubes in 'HOOD' would probably break the ship's back and result in rapid foundering」；并指出 Captain Phillips 的爆炸素描「gives the impression of T.N.T. detonation rather than cordite explosion」。
- **Jurens 用「排气道」把这条异议化解了**（燃气经机舱排气管从主桅两侧喷出），但 2001 年残骸发现也没有找到鱼雷战斗部爆炸的确凿反证——Walker 委员会自己就承认「the possibility cannot be entirely excluded」。

### 争议二：会不会不是俾斯麦号打的？Prinz Eugen 的 20.3 cm 弹

**这是本争议里唯一有明确主流答案、且定量论证完整的子问题。结论：PG 打不出致命效果，但「打不出」与「当时没在打」是两回事，两者要分开说。**

#### 主张「可能是 PG」的

- **Paul Schmalenbach（PG 炮术官）本人说 PG 的目标就是 Hood**：「The Gunnery Officer of Prinz Eugen, Paul Schmalenbach is quoted as saying that Prinz Eugen's target was Hood.」（WIKI-DS:617）
- **hmshood.org.uk 官方站倾向认为 05:56 那次命中是 PG 打的**：「It is believed that either one 15inch shell from Bismarck or one or more 8inch high explosive (not armour piercing) shells from Prinz Eugen struck Hood near the base of the main mast on the port side of the shelter deck. **It is widely believed that the hit was from Prinz Eugen rather than Bismarck.** This conclusion is based on both eye witness testimony as well as the fact that a hit by Bismarck's 15inch shells would likely have caused considerably more damage.」（HOOD2）——**注意这是「起火那一发」，不是「致命那一发」，很容易被误读。**
- 民间与部分海军圈内流传（KB 明确点名）：「It has been sometimes suggested in naval circles that it was the Prinz Eugen the ship that actually scored the fatal hit on the Hood.」

#### 主张「不可能是 PG」的（主流）

三条独立论证，互为补强：

1. **时间线**（KB-DS 脚注 3）：「the Prinz Eugen could not have been responsible for this hit because at the time the decisive hit occurred (0600 hours) the German cruiser was **firing at Prince of Wales**. PG had shifted fire to POW at about 0557 just after hitting Hood in the shelter deck.」
2. **穿深不足**（Jurens，INRO）：
   - 落角：PG 的 20.3 cm 在交战距离上落角仅 **13°–19°**，「an angle which cannot in any meaningful sense be construed as 'plunging fire'」。Jurens 还专门点名反驳过一张流传甚广的图：「one article purporting to reproduce a diagram of the relative trajectories of Bismarck's and Prinz Eugen's guns has been drawn so as to give an angle of fall exceeding 35 degrees. A quick examination of the range tables, however, shows that such an angle is highly exaggerated, to say the least.」
   - 对甲板：着速不超过 **460 mps**，「the official German armor penetration curves for this gun… allow them a penetration of only about **40-60mm** of homogeneous armor at best. In fact, as was the case of the 380mm gun, the curves for these conditions are effectively 'off the graph,' strongly implying almost no possibility of intact penetration at all.」
   - 对舷侧：47° 倾角下「belt armor penetration… would have only been about **100mm for an intact projectile and 103mm for a broken one**, both of which are well under the thickness of even Hood's uppermost and thinnest belt.」
   - 结论原话：「**Prinz Eugen may have been able to hurt the Hood, but she would almost certainly be unable to kill her.**」
3. **弹种**（KB-DS 脚注 3）：「Also keep in mind that **PG was firing HE shells instead of APs**. Prinz Eugen's shells were considerably smaller than those of Bismarck, weighting only 122 kg.」PG 三种弹：Psgr. L/4,4 AP 122 kg / 装药 2.30 kg；Spgr. L/4,7 Bdz HE 122 kg / 6.54 kg；Spgr. L/4,7 Kz HE 122 kg / 8.93 kg。

#### 德国方面的否认

Schmalenbach 对「PG 打了致命那一发」这个说法的原话（德文）：**「Diese Behauptung its eine reine Erfindung」**（此说纯属虚构／无证据根据）。

#### 学界现状

**主流：PG 能打伤、不能打死。** 20.3 cm 的理论穿深差得太远，不是「概率低」而是「机制上不通」。保留的余地只在于「PG 那一发引起的大火是否间接导致了后来的爆炸」——Walker 委员会明确否掉了这条（结论 (3)：「the fire… was not the cause of her loss」），但 hmshood 官方站的表述留了这个口子（「something ultimately caused her aft 15" magazines to rapidly burn or detonate」）。

一个常被忽视的细节：**连「PG 的落弹观察」本身证据都极弱**。Walker 委员会原话：「There is **no very definite evidence of the fall of shot from PRINZ EUGEN**, though one salvo was described as falling astern of HOOD.」（ADM116-89:65）——也就是说，1941 年英国方面连 PG 打了哪儿都说不清，「PG 命中说」从一开始就是靠「Bismarck 那一发破坏力太大不像 8 英寸」这种反推法建立的，不是靠观察证据。

### 争议三：有没有可能被自己人或自己打掉

分三种，都要单列。

#### 3.1 被威尔士亲王号误击

**没有找到任何严肃主张。** 检索了 PoW 71 名目击者的证词全文（ADM116-POW，152,204 字符）、两次调查委员会全部记录（ADM 116/4351）、Jurens 全文、Bonomi 三部分，**没有一处提出 PoW 的 14 英寸弹打中 Hood**。PoW 的齐射落点被反复描述为「over and to the right/aft of Bismarck」（超过并偏右后约 1,500 yd），方向根本不对。

唯一一条模糊相关的证词（ADM116-POW:2371）：「First I saw four shell drop in the water amidships between 'Prince of Wales' and the 'Hood' and the next salvo seemed to fall in the wake of the 'Hood', one shell seemed to hit the quarterdeck.（Witness indicated the starboard side just before 'X' turret.）」——**这是德弹落在 Hood 尾迹里**，不是 PoW 的弹。**结论：未获取任何严肃主张。**

#### 3.2 自己把自己炸了（自舰弹药／炮塔事故）

有主张，但被列为低概率。Jurens 原话：「There remains another alternative, admittedly of low probability, that Hood might possibly have destroyed herself. Evidence is sketchy, but nonetheless does exist.」

他的证据基础是目击者的统计异常：

- 「of the approximately **forty-five witnesses who observed the explosion directly, less than a quarter associated it with a specific fall of shot from the enemy**. A further 22% associated the explosion with the firing of one of the after turrets, and fully **55% of the observers did not associate the explosion with anything at all** - to them the ship just blew up.」
- 爆炸起点分布：「about 42% saw the explosion as originating forward of the mainmast, 16% saw it originating at the mainmast, and 21% either saw it originating aft of the mainmast or someplace else entirely」

具体证人（这是任务书要求「谁主张」的关键，都是 PoW 侧目击者，通过潜望镜看）：

| 证人 | 职务 | 原话 |
|---|---|---|
| P.O. George Henry Goff | PoW 左后指挥仪瞄准手 | 「I saw the third salvo fall… on the starboard side of the 'Hood,' very close. The next thing I remember was seeing flames from 'X' turret. 'A,' 'B,' and 'Y' were firing at the time and I noticed that 'X' turret was not firing… The next thing I noticed was that the 'B' turret was firing but belching out flames. After that 'Y' turret fired on its own and 'Hood' went up.」→ 委员会评语：**「This witness is rather inclined to be imaginative.」** |
| P.O. James Crowley | PoW P.4 炮塔长 | 「I saw a flame come from 'B' turret through one of the guns. Not many moments after that she blew up.」 |
| P.O. Edgar Holt | PoW P.5 炮塔 | 「I saw a flame about 10 feet long shoot out of 'B' turret muzzle and about a half to one minute later 'Y' turret on 'Hood' fired her first salvo. About a second later, 'Hood' blew up.」 |
| P.O. Lawrence Sutton | PoW 上将遮蔽甲板左舷 | 「'Y' turret then fired… and at the same time a huge flash came up all around 'Y' turret.」→ 委员会评语：**「very confused」** |

Jurens 的机制假设：「Could a failure of her gas ejection system have caused a flare back into 'Y' turret which thence blew up the ship?… These possibilities, though unlikely, cannot be positively excluded.」

维基另列一种同族假说（「The ship was blown up by her own guns」）：「At the second board, eyewitnesses reported unusual types of discharge from the 15-inch guns of Hood, suggesting that a shell could have detonated within the gun, causing an explosion within the gunhouse.」

**反证（这条很硬）**：Walker 委员会对上面这批「炮口喷火」证词的处理是**明确不采信**：「Two witnesses were impressed by the excessive flame that once came out of the muzzles of B guns and another reported a similar happening from X guns. **No importance is attached to this.**」（ADM116-89:147）——委员会听到了，记录在案，然后主动判定无证据价值。

另一条反证来自弹库泄压方向：Walker 委员会记录了大量「4 英寸弹药库在爆炸前先泄压喷火」的证词。如果火是先从头弹药库喷出来，那就是「外部命中 → 内部燃烧」，不是「炮塔炸膛 → 往下传」。维基的判读：「the eyewitness evidence of venting from the 4-inch magazine prior to the main explosion conflicts with the theory that Hood was blown up by her own guns.」

**学界现状**：自毁说属**边缘少数**，Jurens 自己定性「low probability」「cannot be positively excluded」，没有任何主流学者采纳为结论。但它揭示的方法论问题是真的：**45 个直接目击者里超过一半根本没把爆炸与任何落弹联系起来**——这是「俾斯麦第五次齐射」这条主叙事最大的证据弱点，值得在报告里点明。

#### 3.3 被自家鱼雷战斗部炸了（DNC 的主张）

Goodall 1941 年的假说，**已被 2001 年残骸证据否证**（找不到鱼雷战斗部爆炸应有的舰体侧向撕裂形态）。维基：「The evidence of the wreck refutes Goodall's theory of a torpedo explosion」。但 Walker 委员会的措辞留了余地：「There is no conclusive evidence that one or two torpedo warheads detonated or exploded simultaneously with the magazines, or at any other time, but the possibility cannot be entirely excluded.」

### 争议四：沉没方式——是「立即断成两段」还是「漂浮了一段时间」

**主流答案：先炸、再断、艉部近乎立即下沉，艏部多撑了数十秒到约 3 分钟。不是「立即断成两段」，也不是「漂浮很久」。**

#### 4.1 1941 年官方口径

- Walker 委员会正文：「Just before 0600 V.A.C.B.S. hoisted a signal for a further turn of 20 degrees to port together, (the range at the time being about 16300 yards) but it was never executed as at 0600 HOOD blew up and **sank in just under 3 minutes**.」（ADM116-89:67）
- Jurens 转述 Walker 委员会认定条目：「**The ship sank in three minutes or less.**」（INRO:259）——注意这是 Jurens 对委员会结论要点的英文重述，不是委员会原文措辞
- Blake 委员会：「After the explosion, which enveloped the ship in flames and smoke, Hood heeled to port, stopped, and **sank within two minutes**.」（ADM116-59:531）
- 海军部 1941-05-24 21:00 公报：「received an unlucky hit in the magazine and blew up.」

两个委员会一个说 2 分钟、一个说 3 分钟以内——这本身就说明「精确耗时不可考」。

#### 4.2 2001 年残骸给出的分阶段版本（hmshood 官方站综合）

原话：「Hood initially split/twisted and/or collapsed into at least two main sections… This damage caused the **stern to tilt upward and sink almost immediately**… **The forward section held out a bit longer - perhaps as much as 3 minutes.**」

Mearns 的沉没轨迹描述：「The stern sank more or less straight down, but the bow followed a glide plane to the east. The midsection, its hydrodynamic hull shape drastically altered, took a southerly glide plane and landed inverted.」

#### 4.3 三名生还者的证词（这是任务书要求的关键部分）

**三人位置完全不同，这解释了为什么只有他们活下来。**

| 生还者 | 全名／军衔 | 爆炸时位置 | 逃生路径 | 看到什么 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ted Briggs** | Ordinary Signalman Albert Edward Briggs, PJX.157404 | **罗经平台**（密闭，舰桥上方） | 从**右舷门**出；Squadron Navigator Commander John Warrand 微笑让路示意他先走；出来时海水已到平台高度 | 「The bows of the HOOD were **vertical with the water about 50 yards away**」「**I was looking at the bottom of the ship**」；先感到右倾 6–7°，随即急剧左倾约 25° |
| **Bob Tilburn** | Able Seaman Robert E. Tilburn, PJX.153249 | **左前方 U.P. 发射器之前的遮蔽甲板下**（boat deck，露天） | 爬到艏楼甲板、跳海；「the water, which was coming over the forecastle, washed me over the side」；被桅杆砸到腿、割掉一只海靴才脱身 | 「when I came to the top just the bows were stuck out of the water, **practically vertical**, and then she slid under」；水中只见「**about 10**」根「Roughly 15 ft. long and 1 ft. diameter」的密封钢管（舷侧防雷系统的 crushing tube） |
| **Bill Dundas** | Midshipman William J. Dundas | **上层舰桥／罗经平台**（密闭） | 海水已到，「when he was halfway through the window, the water came up underneath him」，**踢开右舷窗**挤出 | 「He saw the bows of the Hood at an angle of about **45°**, with the forefoot just clear of the water, **sliding backwards on an even keel**」 |

Briggs 的关键证词（爆炸性质与时间）：「There was **not a terrific explosion** but the officer of the watch said to the Admiral that the Compass had gone and the Admiral said move over to the after control. During that she had listed 6-7° to starboard and shortly after what the Admiral said she listed right over to port. She had gone about 25° to port…」

Briggs 被问「是否减速」时答：「No, we were doing 29 knots.」——**她一直在全速前进，没有减速的过程**，这与「漂浮一段时间」不相容。

Tilburn 被问「爆炸到你入水多久」：「**About a minute.**」——这是最短的个人估值。

**关于「断成两段」这一点，三个生还者没有一个亲眼看到断裂过程**：
- Tilburn 被直接问「你是不是觉得她断成两截了」，答：「**No, Sir.**」
- Briggs：「My impression was as she went over to port, that she was going to capsize but when I came up in the water the bows were vertical. **I would not like to say how she went down.**」
- 断成两段是**德方**（Müllenheim-Rechberg，Bismarck 第四炮术官）先看出来的：「At first the Hood was nowhere to be seen, in her place was a colossal pillar of black smoke reaching into the sky. Gradually at the foot of the pillar, I made out the bow of the battlecruiser projecting upwards at an angle, **a sure sign that she had broken in two**.」（KB-DS）

**学界现状**：分阶段下沉（艉先、艏后）是主流。「立即断两段」是简化说法，实质上是「爆炸→结构失效→艉部沉没→艏部上翘后滑入水中」，全程约 1–3 分钟。精确值不可考，各源在 1–3 分钟区间内分布。

### 争议五：Board of Enquiry 的结论、以及「第二次调查」的真相

#### 5.1 第一次（Blake Board）

- 主席 Vice Admiral Sir Geoffrey Blake K.C.B., D.S.O.；成员 Captain C.F. Gammil（HMS President）、Captain C.H.J. Harcourt C.B.E.（HMS Duke of York）。
- 1941-05-30 开会，1941-06-02 交报告（两页，SECRET），1941-07-10 附证词摘要呈交。
- **结论原话（三条）**：
  - (a) 水上鱼雷战斗部未爆；
  - (b) 「the fire on the shelter deck, although extensive, **was superficial and was not the cause of the ship blowing up**」；
  - (c) 「**the probable cause of the loss of HMS Hood was direct penetration of the protection by one or more 15-inch shells at a range of 16,500 yards, resulting in the explosion of one or more of the after magazines.**」
- 报告里同时写了一句自我限制：「the position round about the mainmast, where the explosion became visible, **has no magazine directly below that point**. The nearest magazine is the 4-inch HA magazine immediately abaft 290 station, that is 65 feet abaft the mainmast.」

#### 5.2 为什么会有第二次（V.C.N.S. 的批评，是理解整件事的关键）

**Vice Chief of Naval Staff（Acting Vice-Admiral Tom Phillips）1941-07-18 备忘录**，两句话定了第二次调查的调子：

- 「I regret to state that in my opinion the report as rendered by this Board **does not give me confidence that such a searching inquiry has been carried out**; in particular the failure to record the evidence of the various witnesses of the event strikes me as quite extraordinary.」
- 「it unfortunately transpired that **no shorthand notes of the evidence were taken**」
- 最见血的一句：「I note that Lieutenant Beckwith gave **hearsay evidence** as the result of having interviewed a number of witnesses. I also note that **of the three survivors from the HOOD only one was interviewed. This strikes me as quite remarkable.**」

hmshood 站的评价：「very few witnesses were called, and of the Hood survivors, only William Dundas gave evidence. Verbatim records of the evidence were not made and to make matters worse, the appropriate experts (on explosives, etc.) had not been called.」（HOOD3:44）

#### 5.3 第二次（Walker Board）

- 主席 Rear Admiral H.T.C. Walker（**胡德号前任舰长**）；成员 Captain R.G. Duke、Captain L.D. Mackintosh D.S.C.（序号页另处写 Captain Morse，档案内部不一致）；顾问 Mr. J.F.B. Carslake（鱼雷／水雷司）、Mr. D.E.J. Offord（海军建造副总监）。
- 1941-08-27/28 与 09-01 在 Dorland House, Regent Street, S.W.1 开庭；另 1941-08-12 在 HMS Devonshire 上开过一次庭。**1941-09-12 交最终报告。**
- **规模**：176 名目击者（PoW 71、Norfolk 89、Suffolk 14、Hood 2）。
- **结论原话**：
  - (1) 「That the sinking of the HOOD was due to **a hit from BISMARCK's 15-in shell in or adjacent to HOOD's 4-in or 15-in magazines, causing them all to explode and wreck the after part of the ship. The probability is that the 4-in magazines exploded first.**」
  - (2) 「There is no conclusive evidence that one or two torpedo warheads detonated or exploded simultaneously with the magazines, or at any other time, but the possibility cannot be entirely excluded.」
  - (3) 「That the fire that was seen on Hood's Boat Deck, and in which UP and/or 4-inch ammunition was certainly involved, **was not the cause of her loss**.」

#### 5.4 两个「第二次」的差异在哪

| 项目 | Blake（1941-06） | Walker（1941-09） |
|---|---|---|
| 证人 | 极少，Hood 侧只 1 人（Dundas） | 176 人，Hood 侧 2 人（Briggs、Tilburn） |
| 证词记录 | 无速记，仅有成员笔记整理的摘要 | 逐问逐答的完整速记 |
| 专家 | 未传爆炸／弹道专家 | Offord、Woollerton、Carslake、Maton/Knight 全部到庭 |
| 沉没原因 | 「一发或多发 15 英寸弹直接穿透防护」 | 「一发 15 英寸弹命中 4 英寸**或** 15 英寸弹药库内或邻近，全部爆炸」 |
| 起火位置 | 主桅正下方（但那里没有弹药库——报告自己承认的矛盾） | 主桅前后**几乎同时**可见（「these phenomena were actually visible in both places almost simultaneously」） |
| 齐射数认定 | 明确第 5 次 | **「第五或第六次」**，不锁定 |
| boat deck 火 | 「superficial，不是原因」 | 「UP 与 4 英寸弹药卷入，但不是原因」 |
| 最终 20° 左转 | 未认定被执行 | 「信号已升，但在 0600 爆炸前**从未执行**」（**2001 年残骸证明委员会此处判断错误**） |

Walker 委员会还有一个方法论上的诚实声明，值得整段引用：「the evidence is largely confused and contradictory… **no witness can be entirely reliable on every point.**」

#### 5.5 「2000 年前后有第二次调查」这个说法

**未获取任何 2000 年前后的正式调查委员会。** 时间线是：1941-06（Blake）→ 1941-09（Walker）→ 2001-07（Mearns 远征，民间，Channel 4 出资）。2001 年那次性质上是「考古 + 取证」，不是法定调查。它在法律上的唯一后果是 2002 年把残骸列为 war grave（受 Protection of Military Remains Act 1986 保护）。

### 争议六：为什么 1,418 人只活下来 3 人

这一条 hmshood 官方站有一篇专门文章（WHYLOSS）给了四因素框架，学界基本接受。四个因素叠加，缺一不可：

1. **爆炸本身的杀伤**：爆炸摧毁「the area of the ship between 'Y' turret and just aft of the second funnel」，两个后炮塔被掀进海里。这一区域的人瞬间死亡。
2. **沉没太快，来不及弃舰**：KB-DS 引德方观察「It all happened so fast that there was **not even time to abandon the ship**.」
3. **下沉吸力**：「Hood generated a substantial amount of **suction** as she sank… They were then trapped under deck heads or pinned to the twisting and rapidly sinking ship by the suction.」
4. **救生装备用错了**：Briggs 把救生衣穿在厚重 Burberry 大衣**里面**——「By having heavy coats and sweaters (not just anti-flash gear and gas masks) over life vests, the buoyancy of the swimming men would have been **drastically reduced**.」其他人没穿大衣的，被吸力压在水下上不来；穿了的上来了也被大衣坠着。

#### 救生筏／救生艇的实际状况

- **救生艇：一艘都没有用上。** Briggs 被问「有没有救生艇」时答：「**There was nothing at all.**」
- **救生筏：是 3 英尺见方的 Denton 小筏，不是 Carley 筏。** WHYLOSS 原话：「each made it to his own **3 foot square Denton raft**. These tiny rafts (which were just a little larger than a life ring) had been stashed all over the ship topside. They broke free in the sinking」。HOOD2 的措辞：「recently installed small grey 3ft square rafts (**not Carley Rafts, but a different design**)」。
- 三人各自抓到自己的筏，然后互相会合。**注意：不是三个人共用一只筏。**

#### 为什么偏偏是他们三个

WHYLOSS 给的解释（这条很有说服力，因为它解释了「只有 3 人」而不是「3 人运气好」）：「they were **propelled to the surface by some release of air far below the surface**, perhaps from an exploding boiler or from air being forced from the rapidly imploding hull… had they been only a few feet from their respective positions, perhaps the air bubbles would not have reached them at all.」

三人的共同点是**都在舰体最高处**（罗经平台 ×2、遮蔽甲板 ×1），离水面最近、上方没有甲板压着。

#### 从爆炸到获救的完整时间线

| 阶段 | 时长 | 来源 |
|---|---|---|
| 爆炸 → 完全沉没 | 1–3 分钟（各源不一，见争议四） | ADM116-89:67；ADM116-59:531 |
| 水面漂浮等待 | 约 2–3.5 小时（**分歧**：wiki 说 2 小时；KB 与 hmshood 说约 3 小时 / 3.5 小时） | WIKI-HOOD；KB-DS；HOOD2 |
| 获救 | 驱逐舰 HMS Electra（Commander Cecil Wakeford May）救起 | KB-DS |
| 送达 | 当日深夜抵 Reykjavik，住院一天后转商船 Royal Ulsterman 回英；1941-05-29 在 Greenock 上岸 | HOOD2；HOOD3:40 |

三人靠**说话唱歌**抗失温——Dundas 不停说话、唱「Roll Out the Barrel」。WHYLOSS 原话：「destroyer Electra arrived just as **at least two of them were starting to pass out from the effects of hypothermia**.」——差一点就变成 1 个生还者。

**「没有尸体」这件事本身就是证据**：HOOD2「There was absolutely no trace of the other 1,415 crewmen alive or dead.」；水面只剩「an oil slick 4 inches deep」和杂物（含一顶皇家海军陆战队帽、一个文件箱）。Briggs 说他周围的油「about 4-6" thick where I was」。1,415 具尸体一具没浮上来——这与「急速下沉 + 吸力 + 重油覆盖」完全吻合。

## 5. 对 `data/battles/denmark-strait.yaml` 现有版本的事实核对意见

现有文件 140 行，整体结构没问题，但**有 9 处需要改，其中 3 处是硬错误**。

### 5.1 硬错误（必须改）

**① 第 123 行 `killed: 1418` —— 错，应为 1415**

1,418 是编制人数（含 3 名生还者），死亡 1,415。三方独立确认：WHYLOSS「1,415 were lost」；WIKI-HOOD「Hood sank in about three minutes with 1,415 members of the crew」；GUARD2001「the 1,415 men who died here 60 years ago」。第 26 行 summary 里「1,418 人阵亡」同样要改成「1,415 人阵亡、3 人生还（编制 1,418）」。

**② 第 89 行「俾斯麦号第一次齐射命中威尔士亲王号舰桥」—— 错，是第 8 次齐射**

Bonomi 逐次记录：「at 06:02, the Bismarck fired her **eighth** complete salvo from 14,000 meters and hit the British battleship on the command tower (compass platform)」（BON2:134）。写「第一次齐射」把整场战斗的时序说反了——第一次齐射打的是 Hood 前方。

**③ 第 93 行「10 门 14 英寸炮仅 5 门可用」—— 错，应为 7 门**

ADM 234/509 逐炮塔清点：A 炮塔 3 门、B 炮塔 2 门、Y 炮塔 2 门，共 **7 门**在射（第 18 次齐射时）。「5 门」这个数出自 Garzke & Dulin 1980 p.190 的「06:13 Y 炮塔卡死只剩两门」，被 WIKI-DS:764 明确否定：「This is not supported by Bennett, Roskill and ADM 234–509.」

### 5.2 数值／表述错误（应改）

**④ 第 61 行「德舰目视发现英舰，距离约 19 英里（35,190 m）」**

两个问题：一是主体错（05:35–05:37 是**PoW 目视发现德舰**，德方同时看到烟柱但晚 8 分钟才拉警报）；二是**括号内换算不自洽**——35,190 m = 21.9 法定英里，不是 19 英里。原始档案是「Enemy was sighted at 0537, range about 38,000 yards」（ADM234-GUNS:57），即 34,747 m ≈ 21.6 英里。建议改成：「PoW 瞭望目视发现德舰桅杆与烟柱，距离约 38,000 码（34,700 m）」；另开一条 05:45 记「德舰拉响战斗警报」。

**⑤ 第 65 行「距离缩至约 26,000 m」**

**没有任何来源支持 26,000 m 这个数。** 各源实际值：KB 23,150 m（25,330 yd）；WIKI-DS 24,200 m（26,500 yd）；Walker 委员会 26,500 yd；ADM 234/509「The true range on opening was 25,000 yards」（22,860 m）。建议改成「约 23,000–24,000 m（各源 22,860–24,200 m 不等）」并在 note 里列出分歧。

**⑥ 第 81 行「至少一发 38 cm 穿甲弹穿透薄甲板进入后部弹药库」**

这是**把两套互相矛盾的学说焊在了一起**，必须改。Jurens 1987 的结论是**否定**穿甲板（落角约 10.6°–13.9°，对甲板穿深仅约 65 mm，而需要 120 mm），他给的通道是「主装带上缘 259–280 站之间约 450 mm 宽的窄缝」或「主装带下缘水下弹道」。只有 2019 年 Jurens/Garzke/Dulin 的 SNAME 论文才改口说穿甲板（且我们只拿到二手转述）。Walker 委员会 1941 年的措辞是「in or adjacent to」，压根没定弹道。建议改成：「弹道未定论：一说从主装带上缘 259–280 站之间约 450 mm 宽的窄缝钻入，一说走主装带下缘的水下弹道；1987 年 Jurens 明确否定『穿透薄甲板』（落角不足、甲板穿深约 65 mm），但 2019 年他与 Garzke/Dulin 的合著论文改为主张穿甲板。」

同句「爆炸在主机与后烟囱之间折断舰体」也不准。断口位置证据指向**后部装甲横舱壁**（Jurens 与 2001 年残骸 Findings 都指向艉部装甲横舱壁处脱离），爆炸破坏区是「between 'Y' turret and just aft of the second funnel」。建议改为「舰体在艉部装甲横舱壁处断裂」。

**⑦ 第 77 行「引发大火，迅速扑灭」**

「迅速扑灭」与三份一手材料冲突：Tilburn 说火「kept on blazing while we were in action」；Holland 的命令是「leave it alone until the ammunition had been fully expended」；HOOD2 说火又烧了「another minute or two」才渐弱，而且是**弹药自行殉爆（cooking off）持续**，不是被扑灭。建议改为「火势蔓延至后部上层建筑，波及 ready-use 弹药柜，4 英寸弹与 UP 火箭弹零星殉爆；Holland 令暂不处置，火自行渐弱」。

**⑧ 第 85 行「3.5 小时后由驱逐舰 Electra 救起」**

这个数**有分歧**，不该写成定论：WIKI-HOOD 说「rescued about two hours after the sinking」；KB-DS 与 HOOD2 说约 3 小时／3.5 小时。建议改为「约 2–3.5 小时后（各源不一）由驱逐舰 Electra 救起」。

### 5.3 需要补／需要调的

**⑨ 第 12 行 `period: 05:52–06:13`**

06:13 这个终点出自 Garzke & Dulin 1980 p.190，已被 WIKI-DS:764 明确否定。可靠终点：**06:09 德舰最后一次齐射**（KB-DS、HOOD2）；PoW 06:10 退出战斗（WIKI-DS:647）；防空炮火 06:20 停止（HOOD2）。建议改为 `05:52–06:09`，或写 `05:52–06:10` 并加注「06:20 防空炮火停止」。

**⑩ 第 52 行 forces「8 × 38.1 cm + 10 × 35.6 cm，仅前主炮可开火」自相矛盾**

「8 × 38.1 cm」是 Hood 的全部主炮（4 座双联），但同一句又说「仅前主炮可开火」——Hood 实际只有 A、B 两座共 **4 门**能打。WIKI-DS:608 的原话是「only **10 of the 18** British heavy guns could train」。建议改为：「Hood 4 × 38.1 cm（仅前主炮塔受射界限制可用，后炮塔在最后左转完成前无法开火）+ PoW 6 × 35.6 cm（前炮塔 A 4 门 + B 2 门）；合计 10 门可射，18 门中 8 门受射界限制」。

**⑪ 第 19 行 `coordinates: 63°22′N 32°17′W` 被当作战斗位置**

这是**残骸位置**（HH01-F：「in the 'vicinity' of 63°22'N 032°17'W, exact coordinates not released」），不是战斗位置。战斗位置用 PoW 05:37 电报的 `63°20'N 31°50'W`（HOOD2）更妥。建议把两个都写清：battle coordinates 用 63°20′N 31°50′W，wreck coordinates 另立字段。

**⑫ 第 58 行 05:30「声呐」→ 应为「水听器」**

PG 用的是水听器（hydrophone）被动听音，不是主动声呐。KB-DS 原文语境是螺旋桨噪声的被动探测。

**⑬ 全部 timeline 条目的 `src` 只挂 `kbismarck-denmark-strait`**

单一来源撑不起逐分钟日志。建议至少给关键节点补 `hooddenmark2`（hmshood 站战斗叙事）、`hoodadm116`（Walker 委员会）、`hmshood-tovey-dispatch`（ADM 234/509）三源交叉。特别是 05:52／05:55／06:00 三个开火与命中时刻，各源本来就有 ±1 分钟分歧，必须多源并列。

## 6. 总结：共识程度、最关键证据、未查到之处

### 共识程度

**强共识（几乎无异议）**：
- 后部弹药库（4 英寸 + 15 英寸）发生了灾难性爆炸，是沉没直接原因。
- 引爆的是**发射药（cordite）燃烧**，不是弹头爆轰——「无声」是决定性证据，两次调查委员会与 Jurens 三方独立得出同一结论。
- Prinz Eugen 的 20.3 cm **打不到弹药库**（机制上不通，不是概率低）。
- 不是威尔士亲王号误击——**零主张**。
- 艉部先沉、艏部后沉，全程约 1–3 分钟；三人全部在舰体最高处、靠 Denton 小筏生还。

**中等共识（主流明确但非定论）**：
- 致命一发来自俾斯麦号第 5 次（Walker 委员会说「第 5 或第 6 次」）齐射。这是主流，但**证据链最弱的一环**：45 名直接目击者中 55% 根本没把这声爆炸与任何落弹联系起来。
- 「05:56–57 那次起火是 PG 打的」——hmshood 官方站倾向此说，Walker 委员会坚持是俾斯麦第 3 次齐射，两说并存。

**真争议（无定论）**：
- **弹道路径**：主装带上缘窄缝 / 水下弹道 / 穿甲板，三说并存。Jurens 本人 1987 与 2019 前后矛盾，这是最需要标注的地方。
- 艏部为何损毁如此严重：前部弹药库二次爆炸（Mearns、Grove）vs 沉没应力与内爆（Jurens、法医组）。
- 自毁说（炮塔事故）——Jurens 定性「low probability… cannot be positively excluded」，无主流采纳。

### 最关键的证据

按证明力排序：

1. **无声**。Jurens 的论证是全场最锋利的一刀：「The 'bang' of an explosion is caused by the creation of a supersonic shockwave, and is necessarily absent when burning occurs.」——它一举区分了「弹药库被弹头引爆」与「发射药大面积燃烧」两种机制，且被 Walker 委员会「The large majority of witnesses heard no noise」独立佐证。**没有这条，整场争论不可能收敛。**
2. **2001 年残骸的舵角**。舵锁在 20° 左转位，证明最后那个转向确实执行了——推翻 Walker 委员会的一个明确怀疑。这是唯一一条「用物证改写 1941 年官方结论」的发现。
3. **112 吨 cordite 的量级对比**。Walker 委员会的原话：「experiments with a maximum of about half a ton of cordite. Hood's after magazine contained about 112 tons, **over two hundred times as much**.」——解释了为什么和平时期的试验经验完全无法预测这次爆炸的烈度。
4. **Offord 的弹道表与 3050 f.s. 临界值**。1941 年英国人手头没有可靠的德弹初速数据，两组数据（2721 / 3150 f.s.）给出的结论完全相反（打不穿 / 打得穿）。这个「数据缺口」是整场争议的技术根源，至今没有被原始德方射表彻底填上。
