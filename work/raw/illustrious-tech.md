## 0. 身份

### 舰级

- **Illustrious class 舰队航母，装甲航母（armoured carrier）**，同级建成 4 艘：Illustrious / Formidable / Victorious / Indomitable。
  - —— ACDES 「This was achieved by an ingenious – and for some considerable time Top Secret - design which incorporated an "armoured box" hangar into a strength deck that was also the flight deck.」
  - —— NPILL 页面标题与分组 「ILLUSTRIOUS fleet aircraft carriers (3, 1940 - 1941)」
- **Indomitable 是改型，不是同型舰**：NPILL 原文把六艘分为三组 「Six ships of Illustrious class became the largest class of fleet aircraft carriers of Royal Navy. They were sectioned into 3 groups and had significant differences. **First three ships had one level hangar and 3 shafts.**」
  - 即：Illustrious / Formidable / Victorious = 单层机库、三轴；Indomitable = 增加了第二层（矮）机库。ACWORTH 进一步给出 Indomitable 上层机库高度：「was as low as **14 feet in the upper hangars on the Indomitable, Implacable and Indefatigable**」。
  - 本条修正任务书中「同级 Illustrious / Formidable / Victorious / Indomitable」的并列写法：Indomitable 与 Indefatigable/Implacable 同属带 14 ft 上层机库的后续形态。

### 舰名来源

- **未获取**（未取得「Illustrious」命名典故的一手原话）。
- 可确证部分 —— WIKI 「**the fourth ship of her name**」；舰训 WIKI 「motto "Vox non Incerta" (Latin: "No Uncertain Voice")」；舰徽 WIKI 「badge: In front of a trumpet erect two trumpets in saltire gold」。
- WIKI 为线索级来源，上述舰名序数与舰训未见一手件核对。

### 舷号

- **87**。
  - —— NPILL 「Illustrious **87**, 1948- R2, 1949- **R87**」
  - —— WIKI 引 Lenton p.713 「pennant number 87」
- 战后改为 R87 的年份：NPILL 给 「1949- R87」，1948 年短暂为 R2。

### 所属舰队

- 1940-08 起 **Mediterranean Fleet**（Force A / Force H 编成内行动）—— ACJUDGE 「Carrier squadron = HMS Illustrious (Flag Rear Admiral Lyster)」
- 1942 起 **Eastern Fleet**，一度为东方舰队航母少将旗舰 —— WIKI 「became the flagship of the Rear Admiral Aircraft Carriers, Eastern Fleet, Denis Boyd, her former captain」
- 1945 编入 **British Pacific Fleet / Task Force 57** —— ACKAM 页与 ACPAC 均以 BPF 语境叙述；具体 TF57 编成日在 WIKI 有 Iceberg I 记载
- 战后 **Home Fleet trials and training carrier** —— WIKI 「she was redesignated as a trials and training carrier」

### 绰号 "Lusty"

- **未获取（1940 年代当舰）**。
  - 检索结果：在 `ac-design` / `ac-judgement` / `ac-excess` / `ac-damage` / `ac-worthwhile` / `ac-saratoga` / `ac-eastfleet` 等 1940–41 一手转录页面中，**没有出现将该舰称为 "Lusty" 的原句**。
  - 已抓页面里唯一出现 "Lusty" 的位置是 armouredcarriers.com 的页面标题 **「When 'Lusty' met 'Sara'」**（ACSARA），属站方 2020 年代行文，不是 1940 年代当舰称谓。
- 该绰号有官方原文支持的是 **1982 年下水的 HMS Illustrious (R06)**，与本舰（87）不是同一艘。
- **结论**：本字段按未获取处理，若下游页面要写 "Lusty"，须标注为「后世纪念性用法／R06 的绰号」，不能当作 1940 年当舰昵称。

### 铺龙骨

- 其他来源：**未获取**（navypedia 无造价列，armouredcarriers 各页无数值）。

### 建造延误

- 装甲板交付延误，其中**飞行甲板装甲向捷克斯洛伐克 Vítkovice 公司订货**

  - —— ACDES 「As a result HMS Illustrious had had her **completion date delayed by up to three months** in order to new masts to be installed, air warning radars fitted – and space found for rudimentary fighter direction facilities.」
  - —— ACDES 「Work on HMS Illustrious had been proceeding on time. But the **late decision to add radar was to delay her completion by three months**.」

## 2. 主尺度

### 全长（oa）

- **229.6 m** —— NPILL 「Length, m **205.1 pp 229.6 oa**」
- **740 ft（225.6 m）** —— WIKI 「o/a **740 ft**」
- **分歧**：229.6 m = 753.3 ft，与 WIKI 的 740 ft 差 13 ft。注意 WIKI 同一段另给「armoured flight deck 753 ft (229.5 m)」——**229.6 m 极可能是飞行甲板全长，而 740 ft 是船体全长**。NPILL 把它列在「Length, m … oa」栏内，属口径混用。两说并列，不裁决。
- 第三方旁证：NPILL 「Later useful length of flight deck was increased to **229.6m**」——支持「229.6 m 是飞行甲板长度」的读法。

### 水线长 / 垂线间长（wl / pp）

- **205.1 m（pp）** —— NPILL 「205.1 pp」
- **710 ft（216.4 m，wl）** —— WIKI 「wl **710 ft**」
- **分歧**：pp 与 wl 不是同一基线，205.1 m（673 ft）≠ 216.4 m。两值不可互换，按不同定义并列。

### 飞行甲板

- **全长 753 ft（229.5 m）** —— WIKI 「**753 ft (229.5 m) armoured flight deck**」
- **最大宽 95 ft（29.0 m）** —— WIKI 「maximum width of 95 ft (29.0 m)」；NPILL 「Flight deck: 190(Illustrious)/204.2(Victorious, Formidable) (useful length) **x29.0 m**」
- **可用长度（useful length）随改装变化**：
  - 建成初期 **620 ft** —— ACDES 「Eventually, the usable length of HMS Illustrious herself was boosted to **740ft. Initially, only 620ft** had been available.」；WIKI 「usable length 620 ft」（因两端 round-down 缩减）
  - 1941 年修理后 **670 ft（204.2 m）** —— WIKI 「the aft round-down was flattened, increasing usable length to **670 ft (204.2 m)**」
  - 1943 年改装后 **740 ft（225.6 m）** —— WIKI 「increasing the usable flight deck length to **740 ft (225.6 m)**」；NPILL 「Later useful length of flight deck was increased to 229.6m」
- **round-down（甲板首尾下弯）是设计特征，不是缺陷**
  - —— NPILL 「The "aerodynamic" form of island and funnel in the form of a profile of a plane wing, and also the round-downs of the deck became feature of Illustrious: it was supposed, that it will decrease the air turbulence and will improve conditions of take-off and landing of aircrafts. At the same time **round-downs shrank deck effective length to 189m**.」

### 最大宽（含突出部）

- **32.5 m** —— NPILL 「Breadth, m 29.2 w **32.5 oa**」
- **95 ft 9 in（29.2 m）** —— WIKI 「beam 95 ft 9 in」
- **分歧**：32.5 m（106.6 ft）与 95 ft 9 in（29.2 m）差约 3.3 m。32.5 m 应为含 4.5 in 炮座突出部（sponson）的最大宽度，29.2 m 为船体/水线宽。两说并列。

### 水线宽

- **29.2 m** —— NPILL 「29.2 w」

### 吃水

- **8.69 m（正常）** —— NPILL 「Draught, m **8.69 normal**」
- **28 ft 10 in（8.79 m，深载）** —— WIKI 「draught 28 ft 10 in deep load」
- **分歧**：navypedia 标 normal，wiki 标 deep load，定义不同；数值只差 0.1 m。

### 型深

- **未获取**（未找到 moulded depth / depth of hull 的直接数值）。

### 干舷（到飞行甲板）

- **38 ft** —— ACDES 「Where Ark Royal had a freeboard to the flight deck of some 60ft, the deletion of the second hangar deck from the Illustrious class in order to preserve stabilitiy reduced this height above the water to **38ft**.」
- **11.6 m（对 Ark Royal 的 17.1 m）** —— NPILL 「reduction of freeboard (**11.6m at Illustrious instead of 17.1m at Ark Royal**)」
- **11.6 m = 38.06 ft**，两源一致。
- 低干舷的代价 —— ACDES 「ILLUSTRIOUS class 'Batch One' key identifying features: **Low freeboard**, large flight deck round-down at rear (early war years)」

### 机库尺寸（本级最核心）

- **长度 458 ft（139.6 m）× 宽度 62 ft（18.9 m）× 高度 16 ft（4.9 m）**
  - —— ACDES 「the vast **458ft x 62ft** vault」
  - —— ACDES 「**The height of the hangar was maintained at the then British standard 16ft.** But … this was reduced a little by the need to stow spare aircraft components overhead.」
  - —— NPILL 「Hangar: **139.6x18.9x4.9m**」
  - —— WIKI 「hangar 456 ft (139.0 m) long, 62 ft (18.9 m) maximum width, 16 ft (4.9 m) high」
- **分歧**：458 ft（ACDES/NPILL）vs 456 ft（WIKI），差 2 ft。两说并列，不裁决。
- **机库宽度的限制原因**（重要）—— ACDES 「the width of the hangar was constrained by the need for the structure surrounding the armoured box hangar to support the heavy armour on the flight deck.」
- **16 ft 高度的后果**（本级被批评的核心）—— ACWORTH 「the armored box hangar on the RN CV's was **restricted to a height of 16 feet maximum** and was as low as 14 feet in the upper hangars on the Indomitable, Implacable and Indefatigable. This restricted the use of the F4U Corsair fighter in the 14 feet hangars. **This also hampered the usefulness of the British carriers postwar as aircraft grew in size.**」
  - 对照 —— ACWORTH 「By contrast, the USN carriers had a hangar clear height of **20 feet in the Lexington class, 17 feet 3 inches in the Yorktown class and 17 feet 6 inches in the Essex class**. This greater height allowed the Essex class to easily adapt to the much larger postwar jet aircraft.」
  - 站方自评 —— ACWORTH 「So far this is the most valid and realistic issue raised. **It is generally uncontested**」
- **16 ft 够用到什么程度**：Corsair 需切翼尖才能入库 —— WIKI 「allowed storage of Lend-Lease Vought F4U Corsair fighters once their **wingtips were clipped**」

### 设计功率

- **111,000 shp** —— NPILL 「Power, h. p. **111000**」；WIKI 「111,000 shp」

### 实测功率

- **113,700 shp（1940-05-24 试航）** —— WIKI 「On **24 May 1940** Illustrious ran her sea trials and her engines reached **113,700 shp**」
- 同级对照（非本舰）—— ACTECH 注脚 「HMS Victorious calculated **31kts at 111,164shp**, HMS Formidable calculated **30.6kts at 112,018shp**」

### 设计航速

- **30.5 kts** —— NPILL 「Max speed, kts **30.5**」
- **30 kts（深载，设计要求）** —— ACORIG 「emergency 30 kts at deep load、operational 29 kts」（WIKI 亦引 30 kts deep load）
- **估价 31 kts** —— ACDES 「Estimates placed her top speed at **31 knots**.」

### 实测航速

- **约 31 kts（推算，非实测记录）** —— WIKI 「Her exact speeds were not recorded as she had her paravanes streamed, but it was estimated that she could have made **about 31 knots**」

- 另一句 —— ACDES 「Average top speeds were in excess of **28 knots**」

### 续航

- **9,250 nm / 14 kts** —— NPILL 「Endurance, nm(kts) **9250(14)**」

- **设计要求 11,000 nm / 14 kts** —— ACORIG 「endurance "11,000 miles at 14 knots"」
- **分歧**：9,250 nm 与 10,700 nm 差 1,450 nm。可能对应不同油量与不同年份。两说并列，不裁决。

### 燃油容量

- **4,840 t 燃油** —— NPILL 「Fuel, t **4840 oil**」
- **4,850 long tons** —— WIKI 「4,850 LT of fuel oil」
- 两值实质一致（4,840 t vs 4,850 LT）。

## 5. 防护（本级最大卖点）

### 装甲飞行甲板厚度——**3 英寸 / 76 mm**

- **76 mm** —— NPILL 「Armour, mm … **flight deck: 76**」
- **3 in NC grade plate** —— ACDES 「the "armoured box" walls were given **4.5in C grade (face hardened)** armour plate and the hangar deck was **3in NC grade** plate.」
- **设计要求原文（1936）** —— ACORIG 「Flight deck. As large an area of the flight deck as possible should be of **not less than 3-inch NC armour**」
- **WIKI** 「flight deck 3 in (76 mm)」

### 是哪种钢、如何与船体结构结合

- **钢种：NC（Non-Cemented，均质非渗碳装甲）** —— ACDES 「3in **NC grade** plate」；ACORIG 「3-inch **NC** armour」
- **结构结合方式：把机库做成「装甲盒」（armoured box），盒顶即飞行甲板、同时充当强度甲板**
  - —— ACDES 「This was achieved by an ingenious – and for some considerable time Top Secret - design which incorporated an **"armoured box" hangar into a strength deck that was also the flight deck**.」
- **覆盖范围与重量** —— ACDES 「**In effect, the 3in armour covered 62 per cent of the flight deck and weighed 1500 tons.**」；NPILL 「**62% of flight deck area was covered by 76mm armour**」
- **两端升降机处不是 3 in 装甲** —— ACDES 「The lifts themselves were not armoured and nor was the flight deck fore and aft.」；「the flight deck around the fore and aft lifts was rated as **1.5in armour – for strength reasons, not as protection**」
- **非装甲区厚度** —— NPILL 「another part of flight deck was **38mm**」
- **装甲盒以外的过渡** —— ACDES 「The "armoured box" containing the hangar space did not extend the full length or width of the flight deck. As a result, the armoured hangar floor **transitioned to 3in plate where it extended outside the hangar and above the 4.5in main waterline belt**.」

### 抗弹设计指标（1936 Staff Requirements 原文，与 ac-design 完全一致）

- **500 lb SAP 炸弹：7,000 ft 以下投掷应能抵御** —— ACORIG/ACDES 「The deck was expected to resist a **550lb (250kg) semi-armour piercing bomb dropped from below 7000ft**」
- **250 lb SAP：11,500 ft 以下** —— ACORIG/ACDES 「and **250lb (110kg) SAP bombs from below 11,500ft**」
- **1,100 lb（500 kg）AP：仅 4,500 ft 以下（且注明为计算值）** —— ACORIG/ACDES 「It was calculated the deck could resist a **1100lb (500kg) armour-piercing bomb only if it was dropped beneath 4500ft**」
- **对 6 in 炮弹** —— ACDES 「the hangar walls would resist fire **from over 7000 yards** and stop 4.7in shellfire **from all ranges**. The 3in deck became vulnerable to **plunging 6in shells beyond 23,000 yards**.」

### 机库侧壁装甲

- **4.5 in（114 mm）C grade 表面硬化钢** —— ACDES 「the "armoured box" walls were given **4.5in C grade (face hardened)** armour plate」
- **114 mm** —— NPILL 「hangar sides: **114**」；ACORIG 「Side belt … should be **4.5-inch C armour**」
- **全机库长度都有防护** —— NPILL 「Hangar sides were protected on **full hangar length**.」

### 主装甲带

- **114 mm（4.5 in）** —— NPILL 「belt: **114**」
- **覆盖范围** —— NPILL 「Main belt protected about **30% of length** by waterline. Main belt amidships was taken to the hangar deck and to the flight deck on the starboard side abreast island. **9m ends of main belt were taken to the upper deck only.**」
- 侧壁 1.5 in 的争议 —— ACTECH 注脚 「** There is some argument that **1.5in side armour** was fitted」

### 横舱壁

- **114 – 64 mm** —— NPILL 「bulkheads: **114 - 64**」

### 机库甲板（hangar deck）厚度——**有分歧**

- **3 in（76 mm）NC** —— ACDES 「the hangar deck was **3in NC grade** plate.」
- **2.5 in（64 mm），并延伸到全舰宽以接舷侧装甲带顶** —— WIKI 「the hangar deck itself 2.5 in (64 mm), extending the full width of the ship to meet the top of the 4.5 in waterline belt」
- **76–64 mm（两舷之间）+ 25 mm（舰体内）** —— NPILL 「Hangar deck had **76 - 64mm** thickness between ship sides and hangar sides and **25mm** inside citadel. Lower deck was 76mm aft.」
- **分歧原因**：ACDES 给的是「机库甲板 = 3 in NC」的单一设计值；NPILL/WIKI 给的是分区实测/图样值（舷侧段 76 mm → 内侧 64 mm → 舰体内部 25 mm）。**三者不是互相否定，而是同一块甲板不同区域的厚度**，但 ACDES 的表述容易被读成整块 76 mm。全列不裁决。

### 弹药库与航空汽油库防护

- **弹药库深埋于舰体核心，被机库甲板与舷侧装甲包裹** —— ACDES 「The bomb and magazine rooms were buried deep within the hull, at the very heart of the ship. Most were encased by the **120 and 100lb NC armour of the hangar deck**, and the **180lb C plating on the ship's sides**.」
- **弹药库顶甲（cap）** —— ACDES 「Protection for the bomb and torpedo magazines themselves was **3in cap protection where the magazine was not beneath the armoured flight deck, 2in if it was**. The spaces also had 2in and 1.5in NC …」
- **航空汽油：coffer dam 系统，悬于水线下海水舱内、装甲之下**
  - —— ACDES 「Large coffer dams were suspended below the waterline in **seawater-filled chambers under armour**. This dramatically reduced the amount of fuel carried for the amount of space dedicated to the fuel systems (**Victorious only carried 40,500 gallons**).」
  - 优点 —— ACDES 「The water pressure helped force the fuel into the distribution pipes. But it also **prevented explosive vapor-filled voids from forming** as the aviation fuel was used up.」
- **机库本身按弹药库对待** —— ACDES 「the Royal Navy … regarded the vast 458ft x 62ft vault to be **in the same category as an ammunition magazine**: A space to be well protected and contained.」
- **机库四周无一处是舰体外板** —— ACDES 「**nowhere was the hangar wall the side of the ship**」；进出只经双重防爆门厅 —— 「access was only through **double-blast-door lobbies**」

### 水下防雷系统

- **深度 4.1 m，可抗 340 kg TNT 爆炸** —— NPILL 「**4.1m deep underwater protection can resist exploding of 340kg TNT**」
- **设计指标：750 lb 装药贴舷爆炸** —— ACORIG 「Under water protection should be provided against **750-lb underwater charge in contact with the side**」
- **750 lb ≈ 340 kg**，两条实质一致，可互证。
- **构造** —— ACDES 「It was provided by **empty compartments** … along with **wing tanks for the fuel oil**.」
- **防破片纵舱壁 1.5 in（38 mm）** —— WIKI 「1.5 in (38 mm) splinter bulkhead」；NPILL 「There were **38mm anti-torpedo bulkheads**.」

### 装甲总重与占比

- **5,000 t 装甲 / 23,000 t 标准排水量**
  - —— ACDES 「**Of the 23,000 tons the Illustrious Class displaced, 5000 tons was armour. 1500 tons of this was on a 458ft stretch of the flight deck.**」
  - 即约 **21.7%**
- **另一算法（Green 的批评）** —— ACGREEN 「The Illustrious class dedicate **2860 t (50% of the total weight of armour)** to protecting the hangar sides and beltline above the water against ship fired projectiles (4.5" face hardened armour is used to defeat gun projectiles not aerial bombs).」
  - 即：装甲总重 5,000 t 中约 2,860 t 用于**防炮弹**（机库侧壁 + 水线以上舷侧），只有少部分用于防炸弹。
- **分歧**：ACDES 给总量 5,000 t 与飞行甲板 1,500 t；ACGREEN 给侧壁+舷侧 2,860 t。两者可并存（1500 + 2860 = 4360，余 640 t 为其他部位），但 ACGREEN 的「50%」与「2860 t」并存时，隐含总装甲重约 5,720 t，与 5,000 t 不符。两说并列，不裁决。

### 与船体结构的重量代价（D.K. Brown 引语）

- —— ACDES 「Weight economy was vital… **The main deck beams were 6ft deep** but, even so, Forbes was to quote a leading structural expert as saying that **he would have used twice as much material**.」

## 6. 航空设施

### 弹射器（catapult / accelerator）——**本级建成即有，不是后期加装**

> **修正任务书前提**：「英国航母早期没装弹射器，靠风」对光辉级**不成立**。1936 年 Staff Requirements 已明文要求一部加速器，建成即装一部 BH-III 液压弹射器。

- **设计即要求一部** —— ACORIG 「**One accelerator is required**」
- **仅装一部的原因** —— ACDES 「**Early designs had two such catapults on the Illustrious Class, but it was observed that they were too close together for concurrent operation so only one was fitted.**」

- **战后换装** —— NPILL 「mid-1946, Illustrious: **catapult was replaced by more powerful**, 30% more aviation fuel was carried.」
- **歧义说明**：above「5.4t plane at 122 km/h」= 11,900 lb at 66 kt，与 ACDES 的 11,000 lb/66 kt 基本吻合。

### 升降机（lifts）

- **2 部，首尾中线布置，无装甲** —— NPILL 「**2 lifts** (6.4t, 13.7x6.7m)」；ACDES 「The lifts themselves were **not armoured**」

### 机库

- 尺寸与高度见 §2。补充：
- **数量：1 层（本级前三艘）** —— NPILL 「First three ships had **one level hangar**」

  - 1941 年修理时改为石棉帘 —— WIKI 「the two steel fire curtains in the hangar were replaced with asbestos ones」

- **拦阻装置型号** —— ACDES 「The **MkIII arrester set** was installed in all wartime carriers after being trialled in Courageous in 1931. The unit fitted to Ark Royal was able to arrest **8000lbs at 60kts**. Later modifications, as fitted to Indomitable, could snare **11,000lbs at 60kts**.」
- **拦阻网 / 防撞网（crash barrier）** —— ACDES 「Aircraft would be parked on the bows behind the protection of a **crash barrier** within a minute of landing as the next aircraft came in.」「**Captain Boyd of HMS Illustrious was the first RN carrier commander to insist upon the use of this method** once he took the helm in 1939.」
  - 即：**Boyd 是皇家海军第一位强制使用防撞网制度的航母舰长**，这是本舰的一条独特记录。
- 「拦阻网」具体网具数量与尺寸：**未获取**。

#### 实战评价——**"rate of fire" 的批评原话**

- 对固定式整装弹的批评（NavWeaps 原文，关键）
  - —— NW45 「**this fixed round proved to be too heavy in service use and there was a tendency for the projectiles to separate from the cartridge cases during normal handling. These problems resulted in a rapidly decreasing rate of fire during prolonged firing periods.**」

- **但 1941-01-10 本舰的实战报告给出的是正面评价**

## 9. 舰载机与编制

### 机库容量（36 架？）

- **设计要求 36 架** —— ACORIG 「**Number of Aircraft to be carried 36 aircraft of present T.S.R. dimensions**」
- **建成后实际机位只有 33 个**
  - —— ACDES 「the Illustrious Class would be limited to **33 internal stowage stations** for aircraft」
  - —— NPILL 「33 aircraft」
  - —— ACDES 「**Many reports state this was 36 or 32**, but a look at the original lash-down arrangements shows provision for **12 aircraft in hangars A and C** … and **9 in Hangar B**.」（12 + 12 + 9 = 33）
  - —— WIKI 「The hangar was designed to accommodate **36 aircraft**」
- **分歧**：设计值 36（ACORIG/WIKI）vs 实测系留位 33（ACDES/NPILL）。ACDES 明确指出「很多资料写 36 或 32」，并按原始系留图算出 33。**这不是错误，是「设计指标」与「实际系留布置」的差别**。全列，不裁决。
- **甲板系留可增加** —— WIKI 1941 年修理后 「carrying capacity increased to 47 aircraft, including 6 parked on deck」
- **1944 年实际运作到 57 架** —— ACEAST 「With a total of **57 aircraft aboard, HMS Illustrious was operating at the edge of her capacity**」「At the height of the operation, Illustrious had **53 of her 57 aircraft airborne**. It took an hour to recover all aircraft successfully.」
- **弹射/放飞效率** —— ACDES 「HMS Illustrious - when carrying a force of 57 Corsairs and Barracudas - demonstrated her ability to **launch 39 in a single range**」

### 1940 年塔兰托时的机群构成

- **Force A 航母中队编成（原文）** —— ACJUDGE 「806 Squadron **15 Fulmar MkI**、815 Squadron **9 Swordfish**、819 Squadron **9 Swordfish**、813 Squadron* **4 Swordfish + 2 Sea Gladiator**、824 Squadron* **2 Swordfish**（* 自 HMS Eagle 借调）」
- **Swordfish 21 架（两个攻击波：12 + 9）** —— 见 §13
- **Fulmar 15 架（806 Sqn）** —— 同上
- **Sea Gladiator 2 架（813 Sqn）** —— 同上
- **总计 41 架（设计编制 33 + 塔兰托特编）** —— ACDES 「This conformed with the design specification… air group of **33 aircraft, rising to 41 for the strike against Taranto**」
- **参战实力：21 架可用的 Swordfish**
  - —— ACTARCO（ADM 199/167，Boyd 报告）「three Swordfish forced landed in the sea on November 9th, 10th, 11th respectively and the aircraft were lost, thus **reducing the number available for the Striking Force to 21**」
  - 原因：燃油污染。同文 「A third obstacle was presented by the discovery that **our petrol was contaminated**, three Swordfish being lost on the preceding days from this cause.」
- **Eagle 未能参战的补偿** —— ACTARCO 「as 'EAGLE', to their great disappointment, was unable to take part in the operation **due to defective petrol tanks, eight pilots and eight observers were embarked in 'ILLUSTRIOUS' from 'EAGLE'**, all being experienced in night flying. **Five of 'EAGLE's Swordfish were also embarked.**」
  - **分歧**：ACTARCO 写 5 架 Eagle 的 Swordfish 上舰；ACJUDGE 写 4 + 2 = 6 架（813 Sqn 4 架 Swordfish + 824 Sqn 2 架 Swordfish）。两说并列，不裁决。
- **长程油箱** —— ACTARCO 「All Swordfish aircraft embarked were fitted with the **60 gallon internal auxiliary petrol tanks**.」；ACJUDGE 「a **60 gallon tank** was secured to the Observer's booth behind the pilot」
- **Swordfish 性能** —— ACJUDGE 「top speed of **143mph** while burdened with its 18inch, 1550lb MkXII torpedo」；带弹带副油箱「maxed-out at just **93 knots**」
- **鱼雷设定** —— ACJUDGE 「At Taranto the torpedoes were set to run at **27 knots at a pre-set depth of 33 feet**… Taranto's battleship harbour had an average depth of **49 feet**.」
- **双触发引信（Duplex Pistol）** —— ACTARCO 「It was decided to run off **100 yards of the safety range** and the battery resistance was removed to ensure that the torpedoes would remain dangerous on completion of their run. The decision to use them was indeed fortunate as the results could not have been obtained by any other weapon.」

## 12. 专节一：装甲航母设计得失

### 一、英国为什么选装甲——战前预期

**1. 直接的设计依据是 1935 年的一份空军判断（Denis Boyd 原话）**

> 「**The RAF assured us that a 500 pound bomb was the largest we would be liable to meet in the next ten years. This was in 1935.** So a carrier was designed to keep out a 500 pound bomb… we were well off the mark.」
> —— ACDES（时任舰长 Denis Boyd）

1936-07-08 的 Staff Requirements 把这句判断写成了硬指标，三条抗弹线全部落到纸面：

> 「Flight deck. As large an area of the flight deck as possible should be of **not less than 3-inch NC armour**」／「The deck was expected to resist a **550lb (250kg) semi-armour piercing bomb dropped from below 7000ft** and **250lb (110kg) SAP bombs from below 11,500ft**」／「It was calculated the deck could resist a **1100lb (500kg) armour-piercing bomb only if it was dropped beneath 4500ft**」
> —— ACORIG、ACDES

**2. 另一条动机是水面炮战，不只是防空**

这是 Andrew Green 对主流叙事的核心反驳：

> 「The Illustrious class dedicate **2860 t (50% of the total weight of armour)** to protecting the hangar sides and beltline above the water against **ship fired projectiles** (4.5" face hardened armour is used to defeat gun projectiles not aerial bombs). **You do not use 3" homogenous armour on top of your hangar and 4.5" face hardened armour on the sides if your main threat assessment is bombs dropped by aircraft.**」
> —— ACGREEN

**3. 关于「挪威战役的教训」——本次未取得支持**

- 任务书提到「挪威战役的教训」作为设计动因，但时间上不成立：Staff Requirements 定稿于 **1936-07-08**，挪威战役在 **1940-04**。设计不可能事后引用。
- 站方对「挪威战役」的表述只出现在**雷达与战斗机引导**的语境，不是装甲设计动因：
  > 「The emergence of radar and fighter direction – developed aboard HMS Ark Royal in combat **off Norway from April 1940** – suddenly restored the effectiveness of fighter patrols and interceptions.」
  > —— ACWORTH
- **结论：设计动因应表述为「1935 年空军判断 + 地中海水面威胁」，不是「挪威战役教训」。** 挪威战役影响的是雷达与战斗机引导，不是装甲盒。

**4. 为什么是「盒」而不是「局部加厚」**

> 「This was achieved by an ingenious – and for some considerable time Top Secret - design which incorporated an **"armoured box" hangar into a strength deck that was also the flight deck.**」
> —— ACDES
> 「The armoured hangar was intended from the outset to **contain the blast** of any bomb or shell that penetrated to prevent further damage to the vital machinery and magazines beneath.」
> —— ACDES

### 二、代价是什么

**代价 1：载机量只有同吨位美日航母的一半**

> 「**Increase of battle resistance was paid by 2 times smaller, than on Ark Royal, air group** and reduction of freeboard (11.6m at Illustrious instead of 17.1m at Ark Royal).」
> —— NPILL
> 「In the late 1930s, The Illustrious' US contemporary, **USS Enterprise, was carrying more than 70, and Japan's Akagi, 90**.」
> —— ACDES

但同页也解释了数字口径差异：

> 「Japan's Akagi and Kaga, for example, are regularly listed as capable of carrying 91 aircraft. But, in 1941, **only 63 were operational aboard Akagi**. Even Ark Royal … had a total stowage capacity of 72 aircraft (though **56 would be classified operational**).」
> —— ACDES

**代价 2：机库只有 16 ft（批评的核心，见 §2）**

> 「the armored box hangar on the RN CV's was **restricted to a height of 16 feet maximum** … **This also hampered the usefulness of the British carriers postwar as aircraft grew in size.**」
> —— ACWORTH（站方自评「It is generally uncontested」）

**代价 3：干舷从 Ark Royal 的 60 ft 降到 38 ft**

> 「the deletion of the second hangar deck from the Illustrious class in order to **preserve stabilitiy** reduced this height above the water to **38ft**.」
> —— ACDES

**代价 4：结构重量代价超出设计者预期**

> 「Weight economy was vital… **The main deck beams were 6ft deep** but, even so, Forbes was to quote a leading structural expert as saying that **he would have used twice as much material**.」
> —— ACDES（引 D.K. Brown）

**代价 5：升降机不受装甲保护，成为结构上的薄弱环**

> 「The lifts themselves were **not armoured** and nor was the flight deck fore and aft.」
> —— ACDES
（1941-01-10 的战损证明了这一点：HIT 4/5/7 三弹都落在后升降机井。）

**代价 6：航速比日本同类低 3.5–4 kt**（见 §14）

### 三、实战证明对不对

**支持方证据 A：塔兰托（攻势）**

- 21 架 Swordfish 一夜瘫痪三艘战列舰（详见 §13）。Boyd 的自评：
  > 「**In one night the ship's aircraft had achieved a greater amount of damage to the enemy than Nelson had achieved in the Battle of Trafalgar, and nearly twice the amount that the entire British fleet achieved in the Battle of Jutland.**」
  > —— ACJUDGE
- **但这条与装甲无关** —— 塔兰托证明的是「航母能打出战略级战果」，不是「装甲甲板有用」。任务书的「塔兰托的攻势 vs 1941-01 的防守」这个二分法需要修正：塔兰托与本级装甲设计**没有因果关系**，它是舰载机战术与夜间训练的胜利。

**支持方证据 B：1941-01-10（防守）**

- 完整抗住 8 枚 550–2,200 lb 炸弹（其中一枚 2,200 lb 直接击穿 3 in 甲板），火灾在 02:00–03:00 前完全控制，主机械无损，自行驶入马耳他：
  > 「**Ship is useless as a carrier until major refit has been carried out. Main machinery undamaged.**」
  > —— ACDAMAGE（ADM 267/83）
  > 「HMS Illustrious – despite being hit by **eight bombs ranging between 2200lb to 550lb** – **never faced an uncontrollable fire. This was the ultimate vindication of her design** and fire-fighting equipment. The sealed hangar isolated the machinery spaces from the bomb blast and spread of its fires.」
  > —— ACDES
- 弹药与鱼雷在机库内被火包围却**未殉爆**：
  > 「**none of the bombs or torpedoes in the hangar space had exploded**… Only boxes and belts of ready-to-used .303 ammunition had "cooked off"」
  > —— ACDAMAGE

**反对方证据 A：那枚 2,200 lb 弹确实打穿了 3 in 甲板（Boyd 本人承认）**

> 「**The only bomb that hit the armour went right through it and exploded on the floor of the hangar. Now had there been no armour that bomb would probably have exploded in the engine room**… The design of the Illustrious shook the world as the words 'an armoured carrier' had a magic of their own, but as you see **it was not quite true**.」
> —— ACDES（Boyd）

即：装甲板的作用是**改变爆点的位置**（从机舱改到机库），不是「挡住炸弹」。这是一个更精确、也更有限的价值主张。

**反对方证据 B：美方 BuShips 1941-06 的冷静评估**

> 「**The fact that the existence of an armored flight deck forces the use of such heavy bombs is an important argument in favor of such a deck. On the other hand, this bomb did penetrate the flight deck and did gut the hangar by blast effect, fragments and fire.** … if this bomb had struck a few feet further forward… it would undoubtedly have **entered a magazine**」
> 「what the results would have been if a ship of the **CV 9 class** had been subjected to the same attack… there seems no reason to believe that the CV 9 would not also have survived」
> —— ACDAMAGE（BuShips 1941-06 报告，Norfolk 期间完成）

**交锋点：两个来源对同一枚弹（No.6，2,200 lb）的入射轨迹判断不同**

> 「The **imputed trajectory of the 2200lb bomb that penetrated the deck armor (No. 6) is different to that supplied to the Royal Navy in the final assessment**」
> —— ACGREEN

这是本主题最重要的一处技术分歧：**同一次命中，美英给出了不同的弹道判定**，直接影响到「3 in 甲板是否被正常击穿」的结论。

**反对方证据 C：装甲盒挤压了攻而非只增强了防**

> 「**Without an advantage in defense, the armor-box layout could not justify its weaker offense.**」
> —— ACWORTH
> 「Lift configuration, freeboard, habitability, ship's speed - the box restricted them all. But the salient fact, overshadowing all others, was the limit it imposed on **air complement**.」
> —— ACWORTH

**反对方证据 D：战后延寿失败**

> 16 ft 机库使本级**无法适应战后喷气机**（见 §2 引文）。这一点站方自己承认「generally uncontested」。同期的 Essex 级靠 17 ft 6 in 机库顺利改装喷气机，Illustrious 1954 年就进了最后维修期，1955 年退役，1957 年拆解。

**站方对「装甲航母无效论」的最后反驳（供对照）**

> 「That Formidable returned to the war zone **two days** after a serious accidental hangar fire and continued to act as an operational flight deck does speak somewhat for itself」
> —— ACWORTH（指 1945-05-18 Formidable 机库火灾，15 分钟控制、55 分钟扑灭、无伤亡）

### 四、本节的判断（按原话归纳，不作史论定谳）

1. **装甲甲板确实救过命**，但它救的方式是「限定爆点位置 + 让机库成为泄压腔」，不是「挡住炸弹」（Boyd 原话）。
2. **它确实付出了载机量与机库高度的双重代价**，且这个代价到战争后期（Corsair/Avenger 上舰）与战后（喷气机）越来越贵。
3. **"装甲航母"这个名号在宣传上比在技术上更成立**（Boyd: 「it was not quite true」）。
4. **塔兰托不能作为装甲设计的论据**；能作为论据的是 1941-01-10。

- 美方观察员 Lieutenant Commander John N. Opie III, USN 全程在舰，提交四页报告附 Boyd 的 12 页报告，分送 King 与 Kimmel —— ACJUDGE
  - 其五点结论：AA fire was noneffective, except psychologically／attackers should fly low to discourage AA／mental strain very great／**ships may be safer at sea than in harbor, especially on moonlit nights**／RN had abandoned high-level bombing against ships

### 一处需要修正的常见说法

- 「海军史上第一次全航母舰载机击败主力舰队」这一表述在本次检索的**原文中没有出现**。可查证的表述是 Cunningham 的「Maneuvre well executed」、Boyd 的「greater amount of damage than Nelson at Trafalgar」、以及 ACSARA/ACJUDGE 的「the first time…」类措辞（未取得完整原句）。
- **建议下游写法**：引 Boyd 那段量级对比，或引「3 battleships sunk or crippled by 9 or possibly 11 torpedoes」（ACTARCO 原话），不要写「第一次」除非补到出处。

### 「英国航母为什么载机少」的直观解释

**一句话：同吨位下，日本拿重量换「两层机库 + 4 万轴马力」，英国拿重量换「5,000 吨装甲」。**

拆成三条：

1. **机库容积差 2.6 倍，不是一点点**
   翔鹤机库 7,000 m² / 33,600 m³，光辉 2,638 m² / 12,928 m³。日本靠**两层 4.8 m 机库**叠出这个容积；英国只有**单层 4.9 m**，而且这唯一一层还被装甲盒的支撑结构压窄到 18.9 m（ACDES: 「the width of the hangar was constrained by the need for the structure surrounding the armoured box hangar to support the heavy armour on the flight deck.」）。

2. **装甲直接吃掉 5,000 吨，而这 5,000 吨在大约 23,000–28,000 吨的船里没有替代空间**
   ACDES「Of the 23,000 tons the Illustrious Class displaced, **5000 tons was armour**.」—— 相当于翔鹤级 32,105 t 满载与 25,675 t 标准的差额（6,430 t）。英国把「多加的那一层机库」换成了「甲板与侧壁的钢」。

3. **航速也一并被牺牲**
   日本为 34 kt 装了 152,000–160,000 hp；光辉 111,000 hp 只得 30.5 kt。ACDES 的设计估价是 31 kts，实际作战条件 30.5 kts。**在 1940 年的地中海，30.5 kt 够用（对手是 28 kt 的意战列舰）；在 1944–45 年的太平洋，这个差 4 kt 意味着跟不上 TF 38。**

**官方的答案（1942 年，最权威的一段）**

丘吉尔 1942-04-14 备忘录：
> 「**What is the explanation why the Japanese are able to carry so many more aircraft than we do?**」
> —— ACCMPJP（ADM 1/11980）

第五海务大臣 1942-04-16 答复四条：
> (i) 「**With the possible exceptions of the SHOKAKU and ZUIKAKU, they are unarmoured and are therefore larger for the equivalent weight.** (Our COURAGEOUS (unarmoured) Class carried as many, of [sic] not more, than the Japanese carriers of equivalent tonnage.)」
> (ii) 「**Their actual tonnage is probably in excess of that disclosed.**」
> (iii) 「**Accommodation for personnel is more congested** than would be acceptable for Europeans.」
> (iv) 「They are possibly carrying and operating a **deck cargo of aircraft** as is the practice of the U.S.A. aircraft carriers.」
> —— ACCMPJP

第 (i) 条是本对照最有力的官方结论：**日方除翔鹤/瑞鹤外都没装甲，所以同吨位下船能造得更大（机库更大）**——而翔鹤正是上表里唯一有 90 mm 甲板的日本舰，也正是唯一被点名的例外。这个自证非常干净。

**注意不要过度简化**
- 翔鹤确实有 90 mm 机库甲板（比光辉的 76 mm 飞行甲板还厚），但那是**单层甲板、双层机库之上**，即「机库顶盖装甲」，与光辉「飞行甲板即强度甲板即装甲」是两种不同解法。ACTAIHO（大凤）页与 ACMID（Midway）页对此有专论。
- 光辉载机 33 是「机库内作战机」口径，美日 84/73 常含甲板系留与备件（ACDES 已就此说明，见 §12）。直接对比会夸大差距，但**差距确实存在**，方向没错。

## 18. 总结

**一、最关键的三个数据分歧**

1. **全长：740 ft（WIKI）vs 229.6 m / 753 ft（NPILL）。** 这不是抄错，是口径混用——navypedia 把飞行甲板全长填进了「Length oa」栏，而它自己在「Aircraft facilities」段写的是「Later useful length of flight deck was increased to 229.6m」。同一页两处自相印证：**229.6 m 是飞行甲板，740 ft 是船体**。下游若只取一个数，务必标清是哪一个。

2. **机库甲板装甲厚度：3 in（ACDES）vs 2.5 in / 64 mm（WIKI）vs 76–64 mm + 25 mm 分区（NPILL）。** 这一处比第一处严重，因为它直接关系到「装甲盒」到底有多厚的顶盖。ACDES 给的是设计单一值，NPILL/WIKI 给的是分区图样值。**目前无法裁决，只能说 76 mm 是舷侧段上限、64 mm 是内侧、25 mm 是舰体内部**——但 ACDES 那句话读起来像「整块 76 mm」，容易被误引。

3. **2,200 lb 弹（No.6）的入射轨迹：美方 BuShips 判定 vs 英方最终评估判定不同。** ACGREEN 明说「The imputed trajectory of the 2200lb bomb that penetrated the deck armor (No. 6) is different to that supplied to the Royal Navy in the final assessment」。这不是数字分歧，是**弹道结论分歧**——它直接决定「3 in 甲板是被正常击穿还是被极端条件击穿」。这是全篇技术含量最高的一处分歧，也是最容易被史家忽略的一处。

补充两个次级分歧：1941-01-10 的**伤亡数（ADM 267/83 的 83 死 / 60 重伤 / 40 轻伤 vs WIKI 与 ACBLITZ 的 126 死 / 91 伤）**——ADM 原件是 1 月 11 日 10:35 的**初步电文**，126/91 是事后统计，两者不矛盾但不可混用；以及**1941 年 281 型雷达是 281M（WIKI）还是 281B（NPILL）**。

**二、哪些字段没查到**

最要紧的三个：**塔兰托当夜的涂装色号**（连"正确色号"本身都没拿到，所以任务书提示的「常被误画」既不能证实也不能否证，只能写未获取）、**HACS 型号**（全库 grep 零命中，只能给 Mk IV 指挥仪 × 4）、**UP 火箭的装舰证据**（NavWeaps 装舰清单与 ADM 1/23040 首批 40 座分配令**双双没有本舰，也没有任何航母**；海军部内部还留了一句「航母应优先于主力舰」的提议，说明这事被讨论过但首批未落实）。

还有两个"任务书前提与史实冲突"的修正必须记下来：其一，**本级建成即装一部 BH-III 液压弹射器**（1936 Staff Requirements 原文「One accelerator is required」，只装一部的原因是两部间距太近无法同时作业），「英国航母早期无弹射器」对本级不成立；其二，**「挪威战役的教训」不是装甲设计动因**——Staff Requirements 定稿于 1936-07-08，挪威战役在 1940-04，时序上不可能。挪威战役在 armouredcarriers 的叙述里对应的是**雷达与战斗机引导**，不是装甲盒。

**三、装甲甲板这一设计到底该怎么评价**

我的评价是：**它对，但它对的理由和当年的宣传不一样，所以它后来输了。**

对，指的是 1941-01-10 那一天。8 枚 550–2,200 lb 炸弹、其中一枚 2,200 lb 直接打穿 3 in 甲板在机库甲板爆炸，机库里 9 架 Swordfish 和 4 架 Fulmar 烧成废铁，**但机库里的炸弹和鱼雷一颗都没殉爆，主机械无损，火在 02:00–03:00 前完全控制，船自己开进了马耳他**（ADM 267/83: 「Ship is useless as a carrier until major refit has been carried out. **Main machinery undamaged.**」）。ACDES 那句话——「never faced an uncontrollable fire. **This was the ultimate vindication of her design**」——我认为是站得住的，因为它的机制很清楚：**密封机库把机舱和爆炸隔开了**（「The sealed hangar isolated the machinery spaces from the bomb blast」）。

但 Boyd 本人给出了更精确、也更有限的版本，这句话比任何后世评价都重要：「**The only bomb that hit the armour went right through it and exploded on the floor of the hangar. Now had there been no armour that bomb would probably have exploded in the engine room**… the words 'an armoured carrier' had a magic of their own, but as you see **it was not quite true**.」——装甲甲板的价值不是"挡住"，是"**挪动爆点**"。它把一次可能炸掉机舱的命中，换成了一次炸掉机库的命中。而机库是可以牺牲的，机舱不行。

所以这套设计的天花板由它自己的前提决定：1935 年空军说「十年内最大就是 500 磅弹」，于是甲板按 500 磅设计；现实送来 2,200 磅。**设计没有被证伪，是被超出了**——这正是 Boyd 那句「we were well off the mark」的意思。

它输在哪里？输在**空间**，不在防护。16 ft 机库是装甲盒结构的直接产物（盒要撑住 1,500 t 甲板，侧壁就得厚、机库就得窄就得矮），而这个 16 ft 在 1940 年只影响 Corsair 切翼尖，到 1945 年影响的是能不能带满 Avenger，到 1950 年代影响的是**根本装不进喷气机**。同期的 Essex 级靠 17 ft 6 in 一路改到越战，Illustrious 1955 年退役、1957 年拆解。ACWORTH 站方自己也承认这一条「generally uncontested」——**这不是偏见，是共同结论**。

所以最公允的一句话是：**装甲盒是一个在"炸弹尺寸可预测"前提下极其成功、在"飞机尺寸不可预测"前提下必然失败的设计。** 它赌对了 1941 年，赌错了 1955 年。而它当年最响亮的宣传——「装甲航母」——恰恰是 Boyd 说的「it was not quite true」的那部分。
